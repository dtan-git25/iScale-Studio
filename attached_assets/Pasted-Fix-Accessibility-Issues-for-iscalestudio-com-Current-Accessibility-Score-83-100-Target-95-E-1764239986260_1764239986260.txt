### 3. VIEWPORT META TAG ISSUE (MEDIUM PRIORITY)
**Issue**: `[user-scalable="no"]` is used in the meta viewport element.

**Current Code**:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1">
```

**Fix Required**:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
```

**Why**: Disabling scaling is problematic for users with low vision who rely on screen magnification.

**Action**: Remove `maximum-scale` and any `user-scalable="no"` attributes from viewport meta tag.
